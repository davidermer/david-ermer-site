The franchise at a glance

1996First season in Baltimore, after the Browns relocated from Cleveland
2 titlesSuper Bowl XXXV (Jan 2001, 34–7 over the Giants) and XLVII (Feb 2013, 34–31 over the 49ers)
M&T Bank StadiumHome field in downtown Baltimore since 1998
The RavenNamed by fan vote, after the Edgar Allan Poe poem. Poe is buried in Baltimore

Franchise greats

  • Jonathan OgdenLeft tackle and the first draft pick in franchise history. Pro Football Hall of Fame, class of 2013
  • Ray LewisLinebacker, Super Bowl XXXV MVP. Hall of Fame, class of 2018
  • Ed ReedSafety and one of the great ball hawks. Hall of Fame, class of 2019
  • Lamar JacksonQuarterback and a two-time NFL MVP, including a unanimous vote in 2019
