Veterans

A page for those who served: where to get help, where to find the benefits you earned, and a few notes on what comes next.

If you are in crisis, help is available now

Dial 988, then press 1

Or text 838255, or chat online at veteranscrisisline.net. The Veterans Crisis Line is for veterans, service members, National Guard and Reserve members, and their families and friends.

If you or someone near you is in immediate danger, call 911.

Transition tips

  1. Translate your service into civilian terms. Describe what you did, what you were responsible for and the results, not just your job code or rank.
  2. Start early. Use your transition program, and look into SkillBridge well before your separation date.
  3. Know your paperwork. Keep several copies of your DD-214 and service records, in paper and digital form.
  4. Sign up for VA health care early. Enrolling before you need care is far easier than doing it in a hurry.
  5. Use veterans’ preference for federal jobs. Learn how it works on USAJOBS before you apply.
  6. Build your network. Veteran service organizations and alumni groups are often the fastest route to a real conversation with a hiring manager.
  7. Give yourself time. The change from a mission-driven team to civilian life is bigger than most people expect. Asking for help is part of the job.
